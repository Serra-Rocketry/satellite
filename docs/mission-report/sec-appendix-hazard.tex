\textbf{Hazard Analysis Appendix}

The following hazards were identified and mitigated by design and/or
process, covering personnel safety during integration, launch and
recovery operations:

\subsection{Hazard 1 — Battery thermal runaway}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Phase:} Flight.
\item \textbf{Assessment:} A Li-Ion cell can enter thermal runaway
  under overcharge, overdischarge, short-circuit or overcurrent
  conditions, potentially damaging the payload or the launch vehicle.
\item \textbf{Mitigation (design):} BMS with multiple protections
  (overcharge, overdischarge, short-circuit, overcurrent); battery
  cell cycle testing before integration.
\item \textbf{Mitigation (process):} battery charged only with
  approved charger, monitored during pre-launch operations; RBF pin
  cuts all energy during integration and transport.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Hazard 2 — Stored energy during integration}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Phase:} Integration and pre-launch.
\item \textbf{Assessment:} a satellite that is accidentally powered
  while inside the deployer can transmit RF, heat up or actuate
  unexpectedly during handling.
\item \textbf{Mitigation (design):} 2 independent kill switches
  (SW1/SW2) in series with the power rail keep the satellite fully
  powered off during integration; RBF pin (SW3) cuts all energy.
\item \textbf{Mitigation (process):} pre-integration verification
  procedure checks kill switch activation; RBF removed only after the
  satellite is installed in the deployer.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Hazard 3 — Sharp edges of SRAB wings}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Phase:} Integration and recovery.
\item \textbf{Assessment:} ABS wing edges can cause cuts during
  handling or during the recovery of the landed satellite.
\item \textbf{Mitigation (design):} rounded leading edges on the
  final geometry; wing covers (stowed) during integration.
\item \textbf{Mitigation (process):} handling gloves during
  integration; recovery team briefed on wing handling.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Hazard 4 — RF interference with other teams}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Phase:} Flight (telemetry).
\item \textbf{Assessment:} Simultaneous LoRa transmissions from other
  teams on adjacent channels can degrade the telemetry link.
\item \textbf{Mitigation (design):} dedicated packet protocol with
  team ID; 915 MHz channel reservation; local storage as data backup
  (LoRa is support, not mission).
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Hazard 5 — Uncontrolled descent}

\begin{itemize}
\item \textbf{Phase:} Recovery.
\item \textbf{Assessment:} In the event of SRAB deployment failure,
  the satellite would descend ballistically, with impact energy
  sufficient to damage property or persons.
\item \textbf{Mitigation (design):} passive deployment with no
  actuators (validated in 2 drop-test campaigns); geometric
  redundancy (4 wings); impact energy within LASC limits in the
  nominal case.
\item \textbf{Mitigation (process):} launch site clearance per LASC
  rules; recovery team positioned per the simulated impact point.
\end{itemize}
