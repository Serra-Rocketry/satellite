\textbf{Abstract}

This report presents the development of the Helike \#213 1P PocketQube
satellite, designed by team Serra Rocketry (IPRJ-UERJ) for the 7th
Latin American Space Challenge (LASC) 2026, Satellite Challenge
category.

The central focus of this mission is the validation of the SRAB
(Bioinspired Autorotative Recovery System, from the Portuguese
\textit{Sistema de Recuperação Autorrotativo Bioinspirado}) — a passive,
parachute-free recovery mechanism based on the autorotation principles
of samara (maple) seeds. The SRAB eliminates the single points of
failure inherent to conventional parachutes (electromechanical
sequencers, pyrotechnics, tangled lines) and offers predictable,
modelable aerodynamic behavior.

The satellite also carries a LoRa communication system with RSSI
triangulation from 3 ground beacons, whose primary function is to
assist in locating the satellite after landing — a recovery support
system, not the mission's main objective.

The report details the system architecture, concept of operations, the
theoretical foundation of the SRAB, the experimental results of two
drop-test cycles, the mathematical modeling and computational
simulation of the descent, the risk analysis (FMECA), and the
pre-flight validation plan.
